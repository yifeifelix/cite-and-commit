**£300,000** — first-time buyers in England pay no SDLT on the first £300,000, then 5% on anything from £300,001 to £500,000. ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax/residential-property-rates))

Two things that catch people out:

- **Hard cliff at £500,000:** buy for £500,001 and the relief vanishes entirely — you pay standard rates on the whole price, so 0% to £125k, 2% to £250k, 5% above. That's a ~£6,250 jump for one pound. ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax/residential-property-rates))
- **These are the reduced thresholds:** they dropped from £425,000/£625,000 on 1 April 2025 and haven't moved since — nothing changed at the Autumn Budget 2025 or Spring Statement 2026. ([Enact](https://www.enact.co.uk/2026/03/stamp-duty-2026-everything-you-need-to-know-before-buying-a-home/), [StampDutyBack](https://stampdutyback.co.uk/blog/spring-statement-2026-stamp-duty))

Same rules apply in Northern Ireland; Scotland (LBTT) and Wales (LTT) run their own systems with different numbers.

Worth knowing: replacing SDLT is live political debate — a cross-party committee asked for a consultation on alternatives before the end of 2026 — but nothing is legislated, so plan around the numbers above.
